## Design

The generator follows a two-phase approach:

1. **Seed preservation**: Loads the 20 seed orders from `seed_core.csv` and passes them through verbatim, never regenerating or perturbing their dates.

2. **Filler generation**: Calculates the gap between seed contributions and target window composition, then generates exactly the required number of filler orders for each window.

### Key implementation details

- **Fixed RNG seed (20260807)**: Ensures deterministic order of generation
- **AS_OF_DATE import**: Properly imports from `src.semantic.constants` rather than hardcoding
- **Date arithmetic**: Uses standard phase durations (STD dict) to walk forward from order_date through the entire order-to-cash process
- **Lead time**: 14 days (order_date to promised_delivery_date) to ensure on-time delivery given 13-day process duration
- **Late orders**: Generated by adding 4 extra days to the delivery phase
- **LF line endings**: Uses `lineterminator="\n"` in csv.DictWriter for cross-platform determinism

### Special cases handled

1. **SO-1009 split delivery**: Two legs with delivery_date 2026-07-16 (on time) and 2026-07-20 (late). Order-grain final_delivery_date is the MAX = 2026-07-20, making the order late.

2. **SO-1015 make-to-stock**: No production_order_id, inspection_lot_id, or goods_movements rows.

3. **SO-1007 cancelled**: Production order with status=CANC, no downstream deliveries/shipments/inspection.

4. **SO-1013 in-transit**: Delivery row with empty delivery_date and delivery_status=IN_TRANSIT.

5. **QL-4004 rework**: Two inspection_lots_raw.csv rows with decision_seq 1 (REWORK, 2026-07-11) and 2 (ACCEPT, 2026-07-14).

6. **Schedule adherence**: 50 of 54 IN-July production orders confirm on or before scheduled_finish_date. PO-3005 plus 3 filler orders (fixed indices 5, 15, 25) are late.

7. **First-pass yield**: 51 of 54 IN-July inspection lots have usage_decision=ACCEPT on first decision. QL-4004 has REWORK, and 2 filler lots (fixed indices 10, 20) have REJECT.
